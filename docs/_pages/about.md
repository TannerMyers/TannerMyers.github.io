I am currently a postdoctoral researcher at the University of Puerto Rico -- Río Piedras, where I am working in the [Papa Lab](https://www.riccardopapalab.com/) under the [E-RISE](https://www.lifeblueprint-upr.com/) Blueprint of Life project (Award no. 2435987).
My postdoc research is focused on the genome evolution of two wide-ranging Nymphalid butterflies: the zebra longwing, *Heliconius charithonia*, and the monarch, *Danaus plexippus*.
Specifically, I am using pan-genomes to study the landscape of structural variation.

In 2025, I completed my PhD at Auburn University, where I worked in the Counterman and Oaks Labs, located at the Auburn Museum of Natural History.
My doctoral research focused on understanding how geographic isolation and environmental variation contribute to the formation of new species using examples from *Heliconius* butterflies and *Anolis* lizards that span the speciation continuum.
